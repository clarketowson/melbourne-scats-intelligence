Melbourne Breathes

Cinematic minimalist electronic soundtrack inspired by Koyaanisqatsi and Blade Runner. Slow evolving synth pulses representing a living city waking up. Deep atmospheric bass, distant industrial textures, subtle rhythmic heartbeat, emotional but intelligent. Gradually intensifies into powerful commuter energy before collapsing into quiet late-night ambience. Massive urban scale, futuristic yet human, transport infrastructure as a living organism. No vocals. Extremely immersive and hypnotic.

Seasonal Metabolism

Emotional cinematic electronic soundtrack representing the yearly metabolism of a giant city. Warm evolving synths, subtle orchestral textures, seasonal transitions, hopeful and reflective atmosphere. January begins quiet and spacious, February rapidly energizes, winter becomes colder and restrained, spring builds momentum, December glows with movement and intensity. Inspired by Hans Zimmer ambient work and Koyaanisqatsi. No vocals. Intelligent, beautiful, large-scale urban feeling.

Top 100 Traffic Empire Reveal

Dark futuristic electronic soundtrack with powerful rising energy as massive transport nodes activate across a city. Pulsing bass, precise rhythmic sequencing, cyberpunk atmosphere, glowing data network feeling. Inspired by Tron Legacy, modern techno minimalism, and high-end sci-fi documentaries. Each new traffic node feels important and dominant, like a giant urban neural network powering up. No vocals. Sleek, intelligent, cinematic.

COVID Collapse & Recovery

Deep emotional cinematic ambient soundtrack about a modern city collapsing into silence and slowly recovering. Sparse piano, haunting synth textures, distant industrial drones, gradually rebuilding into hopeful momentum. Inspired by documentary soundtracks and post-apocalyptic urban atmosphere. Begins tense and empty, then slowly regains movement and energy. Represents traffic collapse during lockdowns and the rebirth of a living city. No vocals. Powerful emotional arc.

Weekday vs Weekend

Stylish electronic soundtrack showing two personalities of a giant city. Tight structured rhythmic pulses for weekday commuter energy transitioning into looser nightlife grooves and relaxed atmospheric textures for the weekend. Sophisticated urban vibe, modern transport rhythms, subtle house and ambient influences. Inspired by modern city documentaries and intelligent electronic music. No vocals. Smooth transitions between corporate intensity and weekend freedom.
